# E-Commerce Customer Churn Prediction

A comprehensive business analytics and machine learning pipeline to predict customer churn.

## Core Capabilities

- **Predictive Modeling**: Employs XGBoost to accurately forecast customer retention and churn probabilities.
- **Data Balancing & Explainability**: Uses SMOTE to handle imbalanced datasets and SHAP (SHapley Additive exPlanations) to identify key feature importance.
