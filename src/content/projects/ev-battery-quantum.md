# EV Battery Quantum Optimization

A research-driven implementation exploring Quantum Machine Learning techniques for Electric Vehicle (EV) battery analytics.

## Core Capabilities

- **Quantum Machine Learning**: Utilizes Quantum Support Vector Regression (QSVR) and Quantum Kernel methods to model complex battery degradation and SOC patterns.
- **Hybrid Architecture**: Combines classical distributed processing with quantum circuits via Qiskit, served through a FastAPI backend telemetry system.
