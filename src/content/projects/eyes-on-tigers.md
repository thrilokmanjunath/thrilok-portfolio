# Eyes on Tigers

A specialized conservation analytics project focused on tracking and evaluating global tiger habitats.

## Core Capabilities

- **Geospatial Analytics**: Utilizes Google Earth Engine to process large-scale satellite imagery and ecological datasets.
- **Conservation AI**: Applies computer vision models to derive actionable insights, culminating in a comprehensive Tiger Success Index for habitat evaluation.
