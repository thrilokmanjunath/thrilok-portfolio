# PaperMate AI (PaperGraph)

PaperMate AI is a comprehensive research intelligence platform tailored for academic literature analysis.

## Core Capabilities

- **Citation Knowledge Graphs**: Maps research papers and their citation networks interactively to visualize academic influence and lineages.
- **AI Literature Reviews**: Automates literature analysis by integrating AI components to synthesize and summarize research contexts.
- **Robust Full-Stack Design**: Powered by a scalable Java Spring Boot backend, PostgreSQL, Redis, and a modern Next.js frontend.
