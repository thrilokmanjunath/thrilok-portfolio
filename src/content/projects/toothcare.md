# ToothCare

An innovative iOS medical software application that serves as a 3D Dental Digital Twin and AI Treatment Planner.

## Core Capabilities

- **Native 3D Rendering**: Leverages SceneKit to provide interactive, accurate 3D dental models on iOS devices.
- **Intelligent Planning**: Incorporates NLP techniques to parse and generate personalized, AI-driven dental treatment plans within a seamless SwiftUI interface.
